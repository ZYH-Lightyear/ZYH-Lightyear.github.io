# Template provenance

This redesign adapts WowPage's Jekyll academic-homepage structure, publication cards,
experience components, and core/full-publication view pattern. Styling and interaction
are extended for Yehang Zhang's Research Neighborhood. The Three.js scene is original
procedural geometry, and all personal content is taken from the existing homepage.

WowPage: https://github.com/WD7ang/WowPage
Reference commit: 162d7dbdb676388a5c6902926e788a994845e42b
Adapted source: assets/css/home.css, assets/js/show_publications.js, _pages/about.md.
